Tips
Drag to paint. Pen pressure changes size and load; pen tilt lays the brush on its side. Fast strokes thin and break up, slow strokes lay more paint; lift while moving to flick. With a mouse, speed stands in for pressure. Hold Shift to keep painting with what is left on the brush (dry-brush).

Watercolour: pre-wet with Clear water for soft wet-on-wet, let colours touch to bleed, drop water into a drying wash for blooms; low Water load gives dry-brush, or Tilt to let it run. Oil stays wet: drag through it to plough ridges and pick up colour; Dry all to lock layers and build on top.

Wheel = zoom · Space-drag or middle mouse = pan · pinch on touch · Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo · Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z redo · B / K tools
